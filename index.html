About me

An educator with a holistic approach to teaching, learning and school development.

Children do well when the adults around them work well together. That means pupils who feel seen, parents who feel heard, teachers who feel supported and schools that run on clear rules.

I have spent over 15 years in primary school classrooms, and I am still hooked. I have taught every Key Stage 2 class, but Year 6 Common Entrance is where I come alive. I have led teams as a Head of Department, helped run schools, built and reviewed curricula, and made technology work in the classroom as an IC3-certified educator. I know what a good school feels like. Now I help teachers, parents and schools build one.

The classroom is where I learn the most, because it shows me exactly where schools succeed and where they struggle.

I pay close attention to the relationships that shape a school day: teacher and supervisor, teacher and parent, teacher and assistant, teacher and pupil. I share what I see in short, thoughtful videos on my Facebook page for teachers, with a little humour so the lesson sticks.

Now I am taking that knowledge beyond one classroom. I help schools train their staff, write their curriculum, policies and handbooks, and set up simple tools such as QR sign-in. I help parents give their children the right academic support. I help teachers grow in their careers.

My aim is simple. Better teachers, clearer schools, and children who are ready for the next step.
